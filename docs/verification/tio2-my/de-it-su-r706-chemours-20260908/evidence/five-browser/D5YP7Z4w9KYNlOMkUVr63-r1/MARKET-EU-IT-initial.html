Prepare the Italy Destination Brief

Enter Italy in Destination Country. If you know a port or city, enter it in the optional Destination Port / City. If a different final receiving site or another handover detail also matters, add it in Additional Requirements.

Before requesting a quote, assemble the context you already have:

  • application and Product / Grade, or state that the Grade is not yet known;
  • required or estimated quantity in metric tonnes;
  • Italy in Destination Country, a known port or city in optional Destination Port / City, and any different final receiving site or handover detail in Additional Requirements;
  • packaging, timing and document requirements, if known; and
  • company and business contact details for follow-up.

You can continue when some destination details are still unknown. Provide the country and the other context already available; do not guess a port, route or receiving site.
