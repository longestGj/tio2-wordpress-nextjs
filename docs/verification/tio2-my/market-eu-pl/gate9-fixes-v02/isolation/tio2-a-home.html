Frequently asked questions

Common Questions About TiO₂ Grade Evaluation

Which titanium dioxide grade is suitable for my application?+

Selection starts with the formulation, process conditions and performance targets. TIOVAR can identify candidate grades for customer evaluation.

Can I request a TDS, SDS or titanium dioxide sample?+

Yes. Specify the product or application context and the document or sample needed. TDS files are provided by request, not as public downloads.

Can TIOVAR help me compare my current TiO₂ grade?+

Yes. Share the current grade, application and target performance so candidate TIOVAR grades can be identified for testing without assuming equivalence.

What information should I provide for a grade recommendation or commercial offer?+

Provide the application, current grade, target performance, expected quantity, destination and any document or sample requirements.
